UI-006 — Waiting, queued, and delayed fulfillment UX with recovery

Status: MERGED — 87b9672 (PR #15)
Depends on: UI-003, UI-004, UI-005
Owned surfaces: waiting/queued/delayed-fulfillment presentation on the track/status surface; recovery action flows (explicit and protocol-authorized); reconciliation visibility for unresolved states; mapping records for waiting and recovery consequential states
Forbidden: protocol semantics; financial authority; bypassing protocol authorization; silent mutation of durable financial state; invented time or progress estimates presented as authoritative

Objective

Materialize first-class waiting UX: when fulfillment is queued or delayed, the user always knows what is waiting, why, what happens next, and which recovery options exist; UNKNOWN states expose their reconciliation behavior; every recovery action routes through protocol authorization.

Acceptance
Waiting, queued, and delayed states are distinct and explicit, and each carries: the reason as reported by the owning authority, the expectation of what happens next, and the available user actions or an explicit "no action is available yet" (P6).
Time and progress claims reflect authoritative information only; the UI presents no invented estimates as authoritative (P6, N1).
UNKNOWN after a wait is surfaced as unknown with its reconciliation path — who re-checks and what triggers the re-check — never as failure or success (P5).
Delayed-fulfillment flows end in explicit terminal states or explicitly-still-unknown states; no dead ends and no indefinite ambiguous spinners (P4, P6).
Recovery actions (for example retry, cancel, escalate) are explicit, individually authorized per protocol, and never silently mutate durable financial state (N3, N5).
Reconciliation visibility: where the authoritative state will be re-checked, the surface shows that this will happen and what the user will see next (P5, P6).
Every waiting/recovery consequential state carries a complete nine-question mapping record (UX contract Section 8).
Waiting states are announced accessibly (for example polite live regions) and remain usable at all shared breakpoints (P9, P10).
Required evidence

Waiting/queued/delayed workflow evidence across outcomes including still-UNKNOWN endings; UNKNOWN-with-reconciliation rendering evidence; recovery-action authorization evidence; mapping-record review; accessibility and responsive spot evidence.

Stop conditions

Any recovery action requiring UI-side mutation of durable financial state or protocol bypass; any waiting state that cannot name its owning authority; any UNKNOWN masked as failure or success; any dead-end or indefinite-spinner pattern on a consequential flow.
