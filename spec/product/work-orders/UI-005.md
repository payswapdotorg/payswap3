UI-005 — Track, status, and evidence surface

Status: PLANNED
Depends on: UI-002
Owned surfaces: universal track/status surface (entry/search, current state, plain-language history); evidence/proof-trail presentation; deep-linkable status views; mapping records for tracked consequential states
Forbidden: protocol semantics; financial authority; bypassing protocol authorization; evidence fabrication, synthesis, or extrapolation; ambiguous pending states

Objective

Materialize the surface where consequential outcomes are tracked: explicit current state, plain-language history, and a complete visible proof trail — with UNKNOWN and waiting as first-class states, and every tracked state mapped to its protocol object and owning authority.

Acceptance
Every consequential outcome materialized by the surfaces built so far is trackable here by an authorized viewer (P7).
Current state is always explicit via the shared primitives: SUCCEEDED, FAILED, UNKNOWN, WAITING, IN_PROGRESS, or ACTION_REQUIRED; ambiguous "pending" does not exist (P4).
UNKNOWN is visibly unknown and carries its reconciliation path: who resolves it and what triggers the re-check (P5, P6).
WAITING entries carry reason, expectation, and available actions or an explicit "none yet" (P6).
The proof trail for a consequential outcome is visible and inspectable: the records behind the outcome are listed with authority, time, and outcome wording — presented, never fabricated (P7, N1).
Unavailable evidence records render UNKNOWN for the record, never a synthesized substitute (P5, P7).
Deep links reproduce the full status view and are role-checked on direct entry (P1, P8).
Every tracked consequential state carries a complete nine-question mapping record (UX contract Section 8).
Timelines and evidence lists are keyboard-navigable with accessible structure (P9); the surface is responsive per the shared breakpoint set (P10).
Required evidence

Track/status workflow evidence across all display states including UNKNOWN and WAITING; evidence-trail visibility and non-fabrication proof; deep-link and role-check proof; mapping-record review; accessibility and responsive spot evidence on the surface.

Stop conditions

Any requirement to fabricate, synthesize, or extrapolate evidence; any tracked state without an owning protocol authority; any UNKNOWN masked as success or failure; any deep link that bypasses role checks.
