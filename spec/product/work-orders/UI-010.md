UI-010 — End-to-end UX closure evidence and Architect closure

Status: PLANNED
Depends on: UI-009
Owned surfaces: the end-to-end UX closure evidence bundle (objective workflow evidence across the composed system; responsive and accessibility evidence roll-up; role matrix; mapping-record completeness roll-up; UNKNOWN/waiting/recovery coverage; environment-signal proof); the Architect closure submission
Forbidden: protocol semantics; financial authority; bypassing protocol authorization; evidence fabrication or extrapolation; anecdotal-only evidence

Objective

Produce the objective evidence that the composed product layer satisfies the UX contract end-to-end — real workflows across all roles and surfaces, all explicit states including UNKNOWN, waiting, and recovery, responsive and accessibility evidence, and complete mapping records — and submit it for Architect closure of the product program.

Acceptance
UI-001 through UI-009 are COMPLETE (Git merge facts authoritative); UI-010's own completion constitutes program closure.
Objective end-to-end workflow evidence: reproducible, scripted workflows crossing surface boundaries (intent, checkout, tracking, waiting/recovery, mediation/dispute where applicable), exercised in the designated environment with tooling, commands, and artifacts recorded.
Every consequential UI state in the product carries a complete nine-question mapping record; the count of unmapped consequential states is zero (UX contract Section 8).
UNKNOWN, waiting, and recovery paths are exercised and evidenced, including at least one authoritative-UNKNOWN scenario rendered as unknown — never as success or failure (P5, P6).
The role matrix holds across all five roles with zero leakage, evidenced end-to-end (P8).
Responsive evidence exists at all shared breakpoints, and accessibility evidence exists at the agreed baseline, for every surface (P9, P10).
Environment awareness is evidenced: the sandbox/production signal is correct, and no sandbox flow is presented as production financial execution (N4, UX contract Section 10).
Evidence is objective and reproducible; anecdotal-only claims do not satisfy any criterion.
Architect closure is recorded through the governed process.
Required evidence

Reproducible end-to-end workflow evidence; mapping-completeness roll-up; UNKNOWN/waiting/recovery coverage proof; role-matrix proof; responsive and accessibility evidence bundles; environment-signal proof; the Architect closure record.

Stop conditions

Any workflow that cannot be evidenced without altering frozen protocol semantics; any unmapped consequential state; any UNKNOWN masked as success or failure; any role leakage; any evidence gap that would require fabrication; an Architect refusal to close.
