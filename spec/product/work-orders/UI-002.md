UI-002 — Customer payment intent surface

Status: MERGED — 5bff0d6 (PR #10)
Depends on: UI-001
Owned surfaces: customer payment intent composition flow (outcome-first entry, review, explicit submit); intent state presentation for the customer role; customer navigation entries; mapping records for all consequential intent states
Forbidden: protocol semantics; financial authority; bypassing protocol authorization; invented settlement or finality language; silent state mutation; optimistic rendering of unconfirmed outcomes

Objective

Materialize the customer-facing payment intent surface: outcome-first composition, an explicit review of full consequences, and an explicit authorized submit — followed by explicit state presentation (acknowledged, failed, or UNKNOWN) in which every displayed state is a presentation of protocol-owned intent state and nothing is invented client-side.

Acceptance
Entry is outcome-first: the customer states the desired outcome before mechanism detail; detail is progressively disclosed (P2, P3).
The review step shows the complete intent and its plain-language consequences before the committing action; nothing decision-relevant is collapsed or hidden (P2, P3).
Submit is explicit and single-intent; acceptance is never fabricated or assumed client-side (N1, N3).
Post-submit state is explicit and uses the shared primitives: acknowledged/accepted, failed with actionable reason where available, or UNKNOWN with its reconciliation path — never a spinner as verdict (P4, P5, P6).
No settlement, finality, or completion wording beyond what the owning protocol authority reports (N2).
Every consequential intent state carries a complete nine-question mapping record; unmapped states do not ship (UX contract Section 8).
The evidence trail is reachable from every consequential intent outcome (P7).
The customer sees only customer surfaces; deep links are role-checked on direct entry (P8).
Accessibility and responsive baselines hold on the new surface (P9, P10).
Required evidence

End-to-end intent composition/review/submit workflow evidence; intent state matrix rendering evidence including the UNKNOWN path; mapping-record review; role-visibility proof; accessibility and responsive spot evidence on the surface.

Stop conditions

Any need to invent protocol intent semantics or state machines; any requirement to compute acceptance client-side; any consequential state that cannot be mapped to a protocol object with an owning authority; any UNKNOWN masked as success or failure.
