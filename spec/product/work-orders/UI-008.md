UI-008 — Agent proposal, mediation, and dispute/recourse surfaces

Status: PLANNED
Depends on: UI-007
Owned surfaces: agent-proposal review/decision surfaces; human mediation interaction surfaces; dispute initiation and recourse-tracking surfaces; mapping records for all consequential mediation and dispute states
Forbidden: protocol semantics; financial authority; bypassing protocol authorization; silent application of decisions; dispute or recourse semantics beyond the frozen protocol architecture

Objective

Materialize the surfaces for consequential human-in-the-loop moments: reviewing and deciding on agent proposals, participating in mediation, initiating disputes, and tracking recourse — with every decision explicitly authorized per protocol, every state explicit (including UNKNOWN), and full evidence visibility.

Acceptance
Proposal review presents complete consequences in plain language before any decision; nothing decision-relevant is collapsed or hidden (P2, P3).
Decisions (accept, reject, counter, escalate, dispute) are explicit single-intent actions routed through protocol authorization; no decision is silently applied (N3, N5).
Mediation and dispute states are explicit via the shared primitives: open, awaiting a party, resolved with the authority's outcome wording, failed, or UNKNOWN with its reconciliation path (P4, P5).
Recourse tracking presents the proof trail for every consequential outcome (P7).
Parties see only their surfaces and their permitted information; deep links are role-checked (P8).
Every consequential mediation/dispute state carries a complete nine-question mapping record (UX contract Section 8).
Decision actions are keyboard-operable and their outcomes are announced accessibly (P9); the surfaces hold at all shared breakpoints (P10).
Required evidence

Proposal/mediation/dispute workflow evidence across outcomes including UNKNOWN; decision-authorization evidence; role-visibility proof; mapping-record review; accessibility and responsive spot evidence.

Stop conditions

Any decision path that bypasses protocol authorization or silently mutates durable financial state; any dispute or mediation semantics contradicting the frozen protocol architecture; any consequential state without a mapping record; any UNKNOWN masked as success or failure.
