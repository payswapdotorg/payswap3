UI-009 — Responsive, accessibility, and role-correctness hardening across all surfaces

Status: PLANNED
Depends on: UI-008
Owned surfaces: cross-cutting hardening pass over every product surface materialized by UI-001..UI-008 at execution time (shell and state primitives, customer intent, merchant checkout, provider capability, track/status + evidence, waiting/recovery, liquidity/credit/queue visibility, mediation/disputes); the hardening evidence bundle consumed by UI-010
Forbidden: protocol semantics; financial authority; bypassing protocol authorization; semantic changes to any surface beyond presentation; silent waivers of violations

Objective

Bring every materialized surface up to the accessibility and responsive bar of the UX contract, verify role correctness across the whole product, remediate every violation, and produce objective tool-backed evidence — without changing what any surface means.

Acceptance
Hardening scope covers every surface materialized at execution time; UI-003 and UI-004 are COMPLETE before this item's acceptance can be declared (scope requirement recorded in the roadmap and ledger, not a graph edge).
Every surface passes the accessibility baseline: WCAG 2.2 AA-level checks (assumption-flagged baseline), full keyboard operability, visible and ordered focus, semantic structure, contrast, reduced-motion support, screen-reader sanity (P9).
Every surface passes the responsive baseline at the shared breakpoints: mobile-first layout, touch targets of at least 44px, no horizontal scrolling, behavior parity (P10).
The role matrix is verified end-to-end for customer, merchant, provider, operator, and administrator: navigation, content, and deep links show zero cross-role leakage (P8).
All explicit-state guarantees are re-verified: the six display states are distinct everywhere, no ambiguous pending exists, and UNKNOWN is never styled or worded as success or failure (P4, P5).
Every violation found is remediated, or waived by the Tech Lead with recorded rationale; there are no silent waivers.
Hardening introduces no semantic changes: state wording, mapping records, and authorization paths are unchanged from their owning work orders.
The evidence bundle (tool outputs, remediation log, waiver records) is produced in a form UI-010 can consume.
Required evidence

Tool-backed accessibility evidence per surface; responsive evidence per breakpoint per surface; role-matrix verification evidence; explicit-state re-verification evidence; remediation and waiver log.

Stop conditions

Any product surface required by the work-item ledger missing at execution time; any violation that cannot be remediated without protocol changes or semantic changes; any accessibility failure that would require weakening explicit-state guarantees; any role leakage that cannot be closed within the product layer.
