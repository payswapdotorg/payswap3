# System completion governance

This is a completion program, not a protocol redesign.

Protocol/backend authority remains frozen `spec/architecture/v0.1/`. Product/UI is governed by `spec/product/`. Deployment/operations are governed by `spec/system-architecture.md`. Cross-layer reconciliation is mandatory through `spec/system-reconciliation.md` and `spec/system-work-items.md`.

The Tech Lead may dispatch bounded workers and drive implementation across product and deployment. The Architect remains the authority for architecture interpretation, review, merge, and final closure.

No completion claim is valid without implementation evidence, integration evidence, operational proof, end-to-end dogfooding, and post-merge state reconciliation.
