# DEP-005 — External rail connectivity boundary

**Status:** PLANNED (dispatchable — DEP-003 + DEP-004 merged: b3bc430, 3126426)  
**Depends on:** DEP-003, DEP-004 (both merged — see spec/development-state/system-program-state.json deploymentProgress)
**Owned surfaces:** production adapter connectivity/configuration and transport safeguards
**Forbidden:** direct provider calls outside adapter boundary; blind UNKNOWN retry

## Objective
Operationalize external rails safely while preserving the frozen adapter, execution, reconciliation and evidence authorities.

## Acceptance
- Credentials/configuration are environment-scoped.
- Timeouts and failures are explicit.
- External UNKNOWN is never translated to failure or success without reconciliation.
- Retries respect existing idempotency rules.
- Adapter activity is observable and auditable.
- Sandbox and production rail credentials/hosts cannot cross-contaminate.

## Required evidence
Timeout/failure tests, UNKNOWN/reconciliation test, credential-isolation proof and adapter observability evidence.
