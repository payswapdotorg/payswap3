# DEP-007 — Observability, resilience and disaster recovery

**Status:** BLOCKED  
**Depends on:** DEP-003, DEP-004, DEP-005
**Owned surfaces:** health/metrics/logging/tracing, backups, restore, replay and recovery automation
**Forbidden:** using telemetry as financial evidence or weakening protocol authority

## Acceptance
- Runtime telemetry distinguishes command, queue, execution, UNKNOWN, reconciliation, clearing/netting, settlement/finality, incident/recovery and deployment health.
- Health/readiness states are actionable.
- Backup and restore are automated/tested.
- Queue and worker recovery are proven.
- Event/journal replay preserves idempotency.
- Evidence integrity is checked after restore.

## Required evidence
Backup/restore drill, worker restart drill, replay/recovery test, evidence-integrity verification and failure-injection results.
