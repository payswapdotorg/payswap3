# DEP-006 — CI/CD and promotion

**Status:** BLOCKED  
**Depends on:** DEP-002, DEP-003, DEP-005
**Owned surfaces:** CI/CD workflows, build/package, promotion and rollback automation
**Forbidden:** bypassing governance, unverified production promotion

## Acceptance
- CI validates source, tests, architecture/governance constraints and deployment packaging.
- Promotion identifies an exact immutable revision.
- Migration steps are gated and auditable.
- Rollback does not replay unsafe external effects.
- Production promotion requires required protocol, product and operational evidence.

## Required evidence
Successful CI run, reproducible build/package, migration gate, promotion/rollback rehearsal.
